**Plans run one Claude per milestone, and Beluga tells you on screen when it needs you.**

- **One Claude per milestone**: a milestone gets one worktree and one Claude that works through its tasks in order, keeping what it learned. It starts with a lean brief, so it gets going much faster. Pass Anyway is instant.
- **A plan graph that reads as a hierarchy**: done tasks shrink to slim rows, each milestone ends in a gate listing its pass criteria, the next milestone stays locked until the gate passes, and passed milestones fold away (click to unfold).
- **On-screen pop-ups when something needs you**: a question, an approval or a decision shows in a small window at the top of your screen, even when Beluga is in the Dock. Try it with ⌘K › Preview Request Pop-up.
- **Tasks for you are decisions, not worktrees**: pick one or several options, or write your own. The plan carries on as soon as you answer.
- **The plan chat is always yours**: send while it works, Stop with ⌘., and it can write. It knows it runs the whole plan.
- **Live activity stays at the bottom of the chat**: what Claude is doing now is always the last thing you see, even after you send it more messages. The working row is cleaner.
- **Files**: search inside files, colors for many languages, an Edit button (read-only until you press it) with ⌘S, and Format with your project's Prettier.
- **Instant archive, delete and new worktree**: the sidebar changes at once; git catches up in the background. Worktrees and chats have Delete… in their menu.
- **Tasks**: proof images survive worktree removal, the task bar stays on top (Esc, ⌘[ or a two-finger swipe goes back), a task links to its worktree, folder and proof, and a plan's badge no longer reads 99+.
- **Sub-agents stay put**: they keep their order, have their own violet mark, and fold away under their worktree.
- **Chat**: drop images from Finder onto a chat, image reads show as a name badge, images in agent activity open where you click, and live tables, charts, stats and code render in chat.
- **Pull requests from Beluga**: commit, create a PR, watch CI in the chat, resolve conflicts with Claude and merge on GitHub.
- **Updates**: "Update automatically" in Settings › Updates; Beluga checks at launch and every few hours.
- **Rooms**: trust choices no longer clash between two Belugas, and you're back in a room within seconds after your Mac sleeps.

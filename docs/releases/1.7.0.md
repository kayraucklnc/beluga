**Beluga 1.7: edit a sent message to rewind or fork, Beluga Link for letting your Mac use your phone, and tasks that tuck under their epic.**

- **Edit a sent message**: rewind the conversation (and its files) to it, or fork a new conversation from there. On the phone, long-press a message for Rewind Here or New Fork.
- **Beluga Link**: Phone Control is now Beluga Link, with its own icon. The phone explains what it is and how to install it, and the Mac sets it up over the cable in one step.
- **Tasks**: sub-tasks start tucked under their epic, an eye on each epic shows or hides them, and they pop out one by one.
- **@ menu**: other projects' tasks only show on a close match, last, under their own heading, and Beluga asks before one goes in.
- **Project home and Work**: Actions, History, Issues, a run and an issue show what was read last time at once, then settle in what's new.
- **Sentinel**: kept tables draw typed cells (documents, progress bars, people, stars, actions), storage objects open in Beluga's viewer, and a resized tile no longer freezes the page when let go.
- **Look**: the light theme has a visible composer edge, an opaque cool sidebar and a crisp dot matrix. A person's name or avatar opens their card, and their picture opens large.
- **Right panel**: no gray gap as it opens, it slides away over the conversation as it closes, and its tab fades in.
- **Phone**: the phone app is 1.7.0.

The Android app (`Beluga-1.7.0.apk`) and Beluga Link (`Beluga-Link.apk`) are attached.

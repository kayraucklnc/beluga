**Beluga 1.5.2: share and hand off a worktree with any friend, and @ a teammate in New Task.**

- **Share a worktree with a friend**: `/invite @friend` in a worktree shares just that worktree over the network, and its Share sheet lists your friends, not only the people in the room.
- **Hand off a worktree to any friend**, no room needed: the offer, its branch and their answer go over your link with them.
- **New Task**: type @ to tag a teammate or friend, and Claude is told who each one is when it writes the tasks.

The phone app hasn't changed: it stays 1.5.1.

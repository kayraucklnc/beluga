**Beluga 1.3: friends and their Claudes work together, team boards across Macs, and your terminal's look comes with you.**

- **Friends, one list**: your room folds into Friends, trust is set on a friend's card, and `/invite` adds a friend (or `/invite @ata` adds them to the chat, project or worktree you're in). Friends' Macs say their Beluga version, so an old one reads as "needs an update", not offline.
- **Share a project with a friend**: it's one row in their Projects with your avatar and its worktrees under it, and it stays while you're offline. A shared worktree is live for everyone in it, and what you say while the owner's Mac is offline is delivered when it's back.
- **@ata is Ata, @claude:ata is Ata's Claude**: mentioning a friend tells them, naming their Claude asks it. Your own Claude takes a real turn with its tools when you ask it in a DM or channel, and approvals show in the chat they're about.
- **Secrets ask each time**: a friend's request for one asks Allow once or Decline, and only that file is sent.
- **Team boards**: friends on the same GitHub repo team up (both saying yes), the board stays in step on every Mac, tasks can be assigned to a teammate or their Claude, plan drafts can be shared for review, and conversations stay private unless you share one.
- **Board**: a parent task follows its sub-tasks to In Progress, In Review and Done, and Tasks open on the board (Kanban) by default.
- **Bring your terminal's look**: import colours, font and cursor from Ghostty, iTerm2, Terminal, Alacritty, kitty, WezTerm or Warp, in Settings › Terminal and the welcome.
- **Create PR, Merge and Update Branch** explain SSH, login and gh trouble in plain words and offer the fix.
- The **/ menu** lists Claude's skills and custom commands; a worktree's sub-agents fold under its own row.
- **Onboarding**: pick the message box's fire (Arcade, Ghost or None) and try it live.
- **Your phone** opens a chat with Claude with the phone beside it, and the live screen stays off until you ask for it. Tasks, Issues and Plans share one Work row in the sidebar.
- **Phone**: voice dictation no longer closes the app, the mic button can't crash it on any screen, and the composer rows fit a narrow phone. The phone app is 1.3.0.

The Android app (`Beluga-1.3.0.apk`) is attached.

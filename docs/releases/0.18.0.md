**Videos play inline on the Mac and the phone, Your Mac gets chat tabs, onboarding walks through each permission, and the phone reconnects, sends and scrolls better.**

- **Video**: MP4, M4V, MOV and WebM play inline in Outputs, the file reader and Scratch (up to 64 MB). On the phone, videos play inline too and stream as you watch.
- **Scratch previews**: pictures, PDFs and videos show as themselves; a video gets its own icon in the list, and a file with no preview says so instead of showing garbage.
- **Chat tabs in Your Mac**: start, switch and delete chats. Your old chat becomes the first tab.
- **Onboarding**: Screen Recording, Accessibility, Full Disk Access and Automation each get their own optional step with a link to the right System Settings pane, plus a short tour and a phone step.
- **Updates**: new installs update automatically; existing installs keep their choice.
- **Installer**: the DMG's arrow points the right way.
- **Phone**:
  - Coming back to the app reconnects at once, and a dropped link retries on its own.
  - The message box takes the full width; tap the send button to queue, hold it to steer; photos are always one tap away.
  - The terminal uses JetBrains Mono, keys respond instantly, and you can pinch to resize text.
  - Big screenshots load with progress instead of hanging.
  - Task keys like BLG-98 become live chips, and sub-agents show in a strip you can tap to follow each one.
  - The branch band is one quiet row.
- The phone app is 0.18.0.

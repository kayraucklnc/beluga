**Beluga 1.1: talk instead of typing, GitHub issues Claude investigates and fixes, your phone from the Mac, your Mac's chats on the phone, and earnings that land with confetti.**

- **Voice**: a mic beside Send and a talk shortcut (hold or tap, from any app; ↩ writes it in). It's written down on the Mac or the phone by Whisper Base, Turbo or Parakeet, with a suggested preset in Settings › Voice. On the phone, speak in Ask.
- **GitHub issues**: Go › Issues lists a project's issues. From an issue, **Investigate**, **Fix It** or **Make a Task**, with a word for Claude; tasks link back to their issue and the project home lists them. A double click sets up one task and one worktree.
- **Your phone from the Mac** (Android): a phone page with a live mirror you can use, files pushed and pulled, the phone's notifications, recorded and replayed macros, and Claude can drive the phone over adb.
- **Stripe earnings**: the project home has an **Earnings** row for the last 7, 30 or 90 days against the span before, with a bar per day and the latest payments. New payments are noticed within 10 minutes, and the sidebar amount hops with pixel-art confetti and gold coins when one lands.
- **Conversations at once**: up to 10, and a setting; a message past the limit says it waits for a free Claude.
- **Fire & Confetti**: never inside the box or cut off. While Typing catches at the caret and spreads round the box, Flame is softer, Pixel Art is smooth, and **Color the Box** is a switch.
- **Toolbar**: folded, its buttons keep a short word next to their icons (PR, Merge, Fix, Commit…).
- The **Tasks** board has the whole window, with no right panel.
- Claude shows a screenshot or GIF it made right in the chat, and a GIF plays.
- Restoring a worktree that's still being archived waits for the archive, then brings it back.
- **Phone**:
  - Your Mac's chats: list them, start a new one, switch and delete, from Home.
  - Plans: the plan's chat on its page with a milestone map under it, and no Approve/Discard before it has milestones.
  - Graphs draw again; a Markdown table with an empty corner is a table, and as cards, long cells show whole.
  - Holding Send opens its sheet over the whole screen, with the keyboard put away.
  - The branch band's ••• menu opens over the screen, in a roomier band.

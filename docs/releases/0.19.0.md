**Beluga opens on friends' Macs instead of saying "damaged", friends' messages show as banners, and run/setup work themselves out.**

- **Installing**: the app is signed, so a fresh download opens after **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security instead of saying "is damaged".
- **Links**: invite and friend links say `beluga://` (old `klaud://` ones still open).
- **Friends**:
  - Friends' messages ding and show a banner while Beluga is in front; your messages show as bubbles on the right.
  - A friend's Claude in chat offers to do things on their Mac as a request, and "Ask X's Claude?" gains **Always** (take it back in Settings).
  - While talking with friends is off, the Friends sheets offer **Turn On** instead of an error.
- **Run and setup** work themselves out: a Makefile or justfile first, else Claude looks once on the first new worktree and remembers it. A failed setup or run asks "Do you want me to fix it?"
- **Views** keep the rows they fetched, so reopening one doesn't run its source again; Claude can fill a view from its own script. Archiving a conversation cleans them up.
- **Scratch**: images open centered and zoom smoothly, viewable files sort first, web pages show, and ← → step through at full size.
- **Toolbar**: on a narrow window the branch pill folds and steps become icons; the panel toggle always shows.
- **Onboarding** reopens on the step it was left on, so macOS's Quit & Reopen after a permission comes back to it.
- Stand-alone chats get their own folder instead of failing.
- **Phone**: Save from full screen, saved files go when their conversation is archived on the Mac, and a new Settings › Storage.
- The phone app is 0.19.0.

The Android app (`Beluga-0.19.0.apk`) is attached.

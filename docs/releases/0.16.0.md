**Your worktrees' files and terminal on the phone, two-way Linear sync, and a heads-up when Claude finishes.**

- **Know when Claude is done**: when a run of yours that took 20 seconds or more finishes, you get a notification with its sound, even in the conversation you're looking at, once nothing else is running or queued there. Turn it off in Settings › Notifications › Claude finished where I'm looking.
- **Files and Terminal on the phone**: a worktree's ••• menu has them now.
  - **Files**: browse, search and read its files, read-only. Secrets stay out.
  - **Terminal**: the same terminal the Mac's dock shows. Type into it from the phone.
- **Linear, both ways**: tasks sync with Linear in both directions, and can keep syncing on their own.
- **Branch state at the top of every worktree**: Merged, Ready to merge or Conflicts, with one click (or a Claude step) to fix it, on the Mac and the phone.
- **Task keys as chips**: a bare task key in your own Claude's answer shows as a task chip.
- **No more main conversation per project**: a project's conversations are the chats and worktrees you start. An old main conversation becomes an ordinary chat with its history.
- The phone app is 0.16.0.

The Android app (`Beluga-0.16.0.apk`) is attached.

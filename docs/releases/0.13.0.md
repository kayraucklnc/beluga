**Beluga for phones: your Mac in your pocket.**

- **Pair your Android phone** in Settings › Phone: scan the QR code or type the short code. Your Mac signs the phone as one of your own devices; your identity key never leaves the Mac. Remove a phone there to cut it off at once.
- **Answer what needs you from the phone**: approvals, Claude's questions, friends' requests and plan decisions, as cards you swipe.
- **See what Claude makes**: conversations, sub-agents and their outputs. Swipe through screenshots and images, and open logs and test output in a reader. Code never goes to the phone.
- **Ask your Mac anything** from the phone, with photos, and chat with friends, asking your Claude, everyone's, or one person's.
- **Easy on the battery**: the phone links straight to your Mac over the same encrypted peer-to-peer connection only while the app is open.
- **A phone mark** in the sidebar's footer shows when your phone is linked.
- **Fix**: a project kept inside a `tmp` folder no longer has all its files treated as outputs.

The Android app (`Beluga-0.13.0.apk`) is attached to this release. Notifications while the app is closed come in a later version.

**Beluga 3.4: no more freezes when a team's board loops, MCP Apps in the chat, share a plain chat with a friend, and a model picker you can search.**

- **No more freezes on a busy team**: one task's activity line could go round a team's Macs forever, freezing Beluga for 10 seconds or more at a time, so a new worktree took minutes to open. A line taken back now stays gone everywhere, a task that loops is held and sent once, and the board opens in about 55 ms. Everyone on the team should update: an older Mac keeps passing the line on to the others.
- **MCP Apps**: a server's app shows under Claude's card in the Mac chat, and anything it does goes through Beluga's trust rules. The phone says which app is open on your Mac.
- **Share a plain chat with a friend**, one chat at a time, from its Share this chat menu.
- **Model picker**: search a long list, models grouped by provider, and DeepSeek and Kimi added. With a local or bring-your-own model, your Claude sign-in never reaches that endpoint, and DeepSeek picks up a resumed run.
- **New worktrees**: your message shows in the chat at once, then Claude starting, and the worktree's `.env.example` catches up with its `.env` names (placeholders only).
- **Plans**: a task that needs you asks with a card, and a stopped one says what it needs, on the phone too. The conversation you plan in runs on the agent picked for the plan's work.
- **Fixes**: a friend's call rings through even while their Mac is busy; a worktree shared on its own stays on its own, and shared worktrees show a green dot; a fork's PR worktree no longer reads as "commits not on PR" after `git pull`; the Delete dialog lets you choose after 10 s instead of staying greyed out; a computer added over SSH doesn't leave ssh running after Beluga quits; chat links are on for everyone; Chinese for what 3.3 added.

The phone app is 3.4.0 too, with the searchable model picker, and it updates by itself.

The Android app (`Beluga-3.4.0.apk`) and Beluga Link (`Beluga-Link.apk`) are attached.

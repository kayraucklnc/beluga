**Claude's answers draw more of what it writes: file trees, JSON, terminal output, math, Mermaid diagrams, and new timeline, heatmap and calendar views.**

- **File trees**: a tree Claude writes (`├──`, `└──`) shows as a compact tree whose folders fold.
- **JSON**: a colored tree that folds, with Tree / Raw, and Copy, Copy Beautified, Copy Minified or Copy Path.
- **Terminal output**: a shell session or command output shows as a terminal: prompts apart, colors drawn, errors tinted, long output folded to its last lines. Copy the output or just the commands.
- **Math**: `$…$` and `$$…$$` are typeset. A price like "$5 and $10" stays words.
- **Mermaid, drawn our way**: flowcharts, sequence, state, ER and class diagrams become Beluga graphs (a sequence diagram steps through its messages); a pie becomes a chart and a gantt a timeline.
- **Timeline, heatmap and calendar views**: Claude gives the rows, Beluga draws bars over time, a grid of counts, or activity by day.
- **The message box**: click an image you're about to send to see it full screen (Esc or a click outside closes it), and images are numbered 1, 2, 3 in each message, even after you remove one.
- Task chips sit on the line with the words around them.
- The phone app is 0.17.0 and draws all of the above too.

The Android app (`Beluga-0.17.0.apk`) is attached.

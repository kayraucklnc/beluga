**Your phone works on any network and does much more. Starting a task takes one click.**

- **The phone connects anywhere**: when a network blocks a direct connection (dorm or office Wi-Fi, strict routers), it goes through a relay. Your phone and Mac still check each other's keys, and the relay sees only scrambled data. When nothing works, the phone says why in plain words and what to try.
- **Watch the work live on your phone**: what Claude is doing, each step with its tool and progress, and each sub-agent with its own live step.
- **Steer or queue** a message from the phone while Claude works.
- **Tasks on your phone**: see, comment on and move tasks; describe one and Claude drafts it; start one in a new worktree with a tap.
- **Merge from your phone**: conflicting files are listed by name, with "Ask Claude to resolve".
- **Everything the chat draws, on your phone**: tables, charts, stats, colours, graphs and the inline tokens. Your Mac fetches the data they need.
- **On the phone, also**: outputs live with their worktree, project icons, "Open on your Mac" brings Beluga to the front, the Ask sheet stays above the keyboard, and the tab badges read clearly in dark mode.
- **One Start on a task**: it creates a worktree named after the task, puts your Claude on it with the whole brief, and takes you into its chat.
- **Steering you can see**: a message Claude hasn't read yet says so, and Send now makes it read the message at once.
- **@task mentions**, task chips that read as pills, the board filtered by worktree, and a worktree's Info tab that lists its tasks.
- **Scratch follows a file's end** as it's written, without flicker.
- **Support Beluga**: star the repo or buy a coffee.
- **Fixes**: a softer light sidebar, pasted images show as named chips, and a carried task links its worktree.

The Android app (`Beluga-0.14.0.apk`) is attached.

**Beluga 3.3: Claude signs in to your MCP servers from the chat, Claude in Chrome in every run, one Project Settings sheet, and draft pull requests when a task reaches In Review.**

- **MCP sign-in from the chat**: when a server needs OAuth (Datadog, Vercel and the like), Claude opens its sign-in page in your browser and its tools load in the same turn. No more `/mcp` in a terminal.
- **Claude in Chrome in every run**: Claude can use your browser in any chat, and asks you first unless you asked for it.
- **Project Settings**: a gear on the project page (or ••• › Project Settings…) opens one sheet for the project. Worktrees get their own variables, and `.env.example` is kept in step.
- **Draft pull requests**: when a worktree's task reaches In Review, Beluga opens a draft PR for it. Merge Now, Skipping Checks also stops the checks still running.
- **Sentinels** run every minute or every 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, on the clock, and a PDF in a kept table opens with its pages, in a sheet tall enough to read.
- **Background fetch, your way**: Settings › Workspaces › Fetch in the background picks every 5, 15 or 30 minutes, every hour, every 4 hours, or Never.
- **Settled chats archive by themselves** after 14 days. Change the number of days, or turn it off with 0, in Settings › Appearance.
- **Sidebar**: colour projects and chats (opt-in), and with the categories on, chat rows can be renamed, retitled and Shift+clicked, the bulk menu has the inbox actions, and settled rows have Archive.
- **In the chat**: selecting text pops up Copy and Ask beside it; Claude's newest in-between words stay in sight while it works and fold away with its answer; sending no longer blanks the chat; tab titles show a named chat by its title.
- **Usage statistics**: a new, opt-in choice (Off, Limited or On, on the Mac and the phone). Off sends nothing.
- **Agents start a task's worktree** from any conversation, and editing a sentinel keeps it on.
- **Fixes**: a fork shows at once and carries the whole worktree, and a worktree being made shows where it will land; the caller hears the ringback again; call pointers fold away when idle; right-click menus close on any outside press, one at a time; pop-up Later never opens Beluga; opening a content search hit no longer shifts the window; What Waits on What stays smooth on a real-sized board; a plan gate's "Checked · pass it" passes the milestone.

The phone app is 3.3.0 too, with Usage statistics in its Settings, and it updates by itself.

The Android app (`Beluga-3.3.0.apk`) and Beluga Link (`Beluga-Link.apk`) are attached.

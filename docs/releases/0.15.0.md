**Talk to your Mac from your phone, and start real work there.**

- **Your Mac**: a conversation with your Mac itself, the first choice under + on the phone and at the top of the Mac's sidebar. Ask it to take a screenshot and send it to you, tell you what's using the CPU, run something, or start a worktree, task or plan. What it starts appears as links you can tap. It uses your own Claude, with your usual approvals.
- **Start real work in a project from the phone**:
  - **Worktree**, with a Plan first switch.
  - **Advanced plan**: milestones, tasks, dependencies and criteria, which you approve, set the autonomy of, or discard on the phone. You can follow it live as it runs.
  - **Question**, read-only.
- **Tables Claude writes as text** show as real tables on the phone. Every table, chart and graph can go full screen, also sideways, and wide tables can switch to one card per row.
- **Outputs at the end of a turn** are a compact strip you swipe, instead of big blocks.
- **The phone no longer lists a project's main conversation.**
- **Image chips in chat**: only as wide as their name, long names cut at the start (…8.14.32@2x), and an image at the very start of a message no longer goes missing.
- **Pair a Phone** keeps its shape with a spinner while it opens, and the QR code appears sooner.
- The phone app's version now matches Beluga's: 0.15.0.

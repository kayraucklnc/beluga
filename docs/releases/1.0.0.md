**Beluga 1.0: the first full release. Create PR in one click, Merge When Ready that really waits, a phone that stays connected, and a message box that can catch fire.**

- **Create PR** opens the pull request right away, no Claude needed. The chevron next to it has **Create PR with Claude**, **with Instructions…** and **Draft PR**, on the Mac and the phone.
- **Merge When Ready** waits for the checks itself and merges once they pass. It stops with a notification if a check fails, the PR conflicts or changes are requested, and **Cancel Auto-Merge** takes it back. It picks up again after a restart.
- **Phone connection**:
  - The relay heals itself after the Mac sleeps, so the phone reconnects within about a minute instead of saying it can't connect.
  - Both screens say what's wrong: "Reconnecting to your Mac…" on the phone, **Pair again** if the Mac forgot it, and Settings › Phone on the Mac shows whether phones on other networks get through.
  - **Keep this Mac awake**: Never, On power or Always, set from the Mac or the phone.
  - **Stay connected** (Android, opt-in): the link survives the screen going off, and "Claude finished" arrives as a notification.
- **Phone**: add photos when starting a new worktree, and the branch bar's ••• is no longer cut off or drawn under the chat.
- **Plan with Tasks** only makes its plan when you send, and the plan-mode box is calmer: a thin pastel border and a small tag (teal for Plan First, lavender for Plan with Tasks).
- **Fire & Confetti**: the message box can burn (Flame or Pixel Art), typing throws confetti and deleting pops the text into pixels. Presets, sliders and your own colours in Settings › Fire & Confetti; on Max only by default, and it respects Reduce Motion.
- Each Claude's chat updates stay in its own conversation when several run at once.
- The full-size image viewer opens pictures centered instead of off at the bottom of the window.
- The phone app is 1.0.0.

The Android app (`Beluga-1.0.0.apk`) is attached.

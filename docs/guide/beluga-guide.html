Beluga,
the short guide

A Mac app where every task gets its own worktree and its own Claude, and where your Claude can ask a friend's Claude to do something on their Mac. Here are the cool parts, the way people actually use them.

  • One worktree and one Claude per task, all at once
  • Answers that show: charts, tables, diagrams, task cards
  • Ask a friend's Claude, Mac to Mac, after they click Allow
  • Sentinels that keep watch and fix things while you sleep
  • Your phone sees what needs you, and answers it

A worktree: the conversation in the middle, its changes on the right, projects and friends on the left.

The map

Everything Beluga has, on one page

Six branches. Each one is a place in the sidebar, and each one also reaches your phone.

Worktrees

  • A task, a worktree, a Claude: own folder and branch
  • Many at once, with sub-agents you can watch
  • Right panel: Changes, Files, Scratch, Info
  • Terminals beside the chat (⌘J)
  • Commit, PR, Merge in one click
  • Plan / Act, model and thinking per chat
  • Edit a sent message to rewind or fork

Answers that show

  • Charts and stat tiles: line, bar, pie, gantt, heatmap
  • Live tables paged from a file, DB, URL or command
  • Diagrams with a step-by-step walkthrough
  • Task cards with live status
  • Markdown: math, Mermaid, folding JSON
  • Scratch: screenshots, logs, JSON as Claude works

Tasks and Plans

  • A board per project: list, kanban, graph
  • Brief: goal, criteria, decisions, handoff
  • Epics and sub-tasks, labels, @-mentions
  • Done when its worktree merges
  • Plans: milestones a head Claude runs
  • Linear and GitHub issues sync, shared boards

Friends

  • @-tag a friend: your Claude asks theirs
  • Allow / Allow Always / Decline / See more
  • Trust levels: Ask me each time, Trusted, Full trust
  • Files, screenshots, commands, device info
  • Chats and groups, end-to-end encrypted
  • Peer to peer: no server, no account

Sentinel

  • When: a schedule, GitHub, Stripe, Sentry, uptime, webhook
  • Steps: ask Claude, script, PR, merge, notify
  • Eleven recipes: Morning brief, Fix what broke…
  • Dashboard of kept numbers and tables
  • Safe: starts paused, merge needs your tap

Your Mac and phone

  • Activity: every Claude Code session on the Mac
  • Usage: plan limits, tokens, disk per project
  • Phone app: NeedsYou, chats, files, terminal
  • Beluga Link: your Mac drives your phone
  • Menu bar, ⌘K, notifications, self-updating

Use case

"Do my tests pass on your machine?"

You changed the webhooks and want to know if bob's checkout of bob-api still passes. Instead of a Slack thread, you tag bob.

@bob run the tests in bob-api and tell me how many pass

Your Claude finds bob, asks his Claude, and waits. bob gets a small pop-up wherever they are, reads it with See more, and clicks Allow. Their Claude runs the tests on their Mac and the result comes back as a table.

  • No server and no shared account: Mac to Mac, end-to-end encrypted
  • Each Claude uses its owner's subscription and logins
  • Trusted friends skip the pop-up; secrets are refused at every level
  • Also: "send me a screenshot", "send me that PDF", "make me admin on the repo"

Use case

CI broke at 3 am. It's fixed by breakfast.

The Fix what broke recipe watches GitHub. When a workflow fails on main, Claude finds the cause in a fresh worktree, opens a pull request and waits for the checks and a reviewer.

In the morning there's one thing waiting: a Merge button, on the Mac or the phone, with a short report of what broke and why the fix is right.

  • Triggers: a schedule, GitHub, Stripe, Sentry, a site going down, a webhook
  • Starts paused; Look, Work and Ship say how far Claude may go
  • Morning brief: every day at 7, what changed, sent to your phone
  • Keep the numbers: runs feed tiles on Sentinel's dashboard

Use case

"How does a payment flow?" and "How are payments doing?"

New to a codebase, or checking on the business: ask, and the answer is something you can click, not a wall of text.

A diagram with a walkthrough

/visualize draws the flow from the checkout page through the API, Stripe and the webhook to the orders table. Hover a part to see what it touches, then step through it.

A live dashboard in the chat

Stat tiles, a chart of payments per day and a table of the latest orders that pages, sorts and searches straight from the source, without asking Claude again.

Use case

Four Claudes running, and you're at lunch

Before you leave, Activity shows everything at work on your Mac: every Claude Code session, Beluga's or not, and the background agents each one started.

Usage tells you if there's room for one more: how much of your plan's 5-hour and weekly limits is left, tokens per day, and which projects' old worktrees you can clear up in one click.

Then you go. When a Claude has a question, a friend asks for something or a fix waits for Merge, your phone buzzes. You answer it from the phone, and the work carries on.

  • NeedsYou on the phone: approvals, Claude's questions, friends' requests
  • Read a worktree's files and use its terminal from the phone
  • Start new work from the phone: a question, a task, a new worktree
  • The menu-bar whale spouts while Claude works, and its number shows what's new
